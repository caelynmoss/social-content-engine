# Information Gap Mining — earn a non-obvious insight

Goal: discover **consequential, checkable information** behind a topic that the draft otherwise lacks. “CT overlooked this” or “nobody is talking about it” are *comparative empirical claims*: make them only when accessible sampled CT posts substantiate the comparison. Otherwise write “an angle worth checking,” not a proven blind spot.

## Look under the surface

Select 1–3 focused investigation paths based on the account's domain:

- **Mechanism / authority:** actual contract ownership, multisig keys, revocation rights, governance parameters, token supply rules; validate chain/version/block date.
- **Incentives and distribution:** fee beneficiaries, unlocks, liquidity control, real user costs, who bears risk, eligibility details.
- **Usage vs narrative:** public promises compared with official documentation, independent data, active users, transactions, policy changes; check definitions and sampling windows.
- **Second-order effect:** the choice changed for a specific builder/user/trader, not merely a “future of X” slogan.
- **Negative evidence:** known counterexamples, failures, restatements, inconsistencies, limitations or an explicit gap in available measurement.

## Information Gain Card (make silently for short posts, optionally surface for research requests)

1. **Common narrative:** what the sampled sources actually say, linked and dated.
2. **Candidate overlooked detail:** an explicit fact or testable mechanism, not gossip or a mystery-box hook.
3. **Why it changes a decision:** whom it matters to and the consequence if true.
4. **Source and verification status:** primary URL / document path, observed timestamp or block, and any missing comparison.
5. **Potential original thesis:** label clearly as SUGGESTED INFERENCE until the author adopts it.
6. **Credible counterpoint:** what would weaken the thesis or show another explanation.

For a deep Article, attempt 2–3 strong cards **only when real evidence supports them**. For a short post use the strongest single insight rather than cramming 5 facts. No fabricated novelty metrics, X engagement counts, unpublished contracts or privileged information. If research sources are unavailable, give an explicit *research plan / questions to verify* rather than fictional information gain.
