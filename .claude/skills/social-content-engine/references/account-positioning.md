# Account positioning: an editorial constitution before any angle

**Sequence: choose account -> load account charter -> inspect account's winning format -> decide owner and channel -> select angle -> verify claims -> draft in the author's voice.** A voice card records HOW someone writes; an Account Profile records WHAT this account stands for, whom it serves and where it will not go. Keep these two documents separate and linked by account ID. See `assets/account-profile-template.md`.

## Account onboarding (first time, not before every one-off draft)

Use owner-approved evidence and short conversational questions, not a lengthy form. Capture:

1. **Format that wins here -- FIRST LINE.** Record leading format with metric, time window, number of posts, comparator and confidence. It may be Article, a long single post, short post, thread or an image/video + caption. If no trustworthy data exists, write `UNKNOWN / TEST NEEDED`, never assume Short Post or Thread. Always obey an explicit current format request.
2. Identity: account ID, account type (personal / official / other), intended audience, audience promise and goal; don't infer ownership from X handles.
3. Point of view: core lens, main beliefs *as explicitly supplied*, what the account opposes, recurring editorial questions, acceptable adjacent subjects, authenticity boundaries. Positioning is more useful than a rigid niche; keep it editable.
4. Vocabulary: preferred terms/translations, banned phrases, jargon level, slang, first-person policy, proof style, tone and editorial red lines. Do not invent an account's political, financial or other sensitive stances.
5. **Approved source register:** private, authorized local path(s) or URLs for factual claims, approver/status, release scope and last-verified date. Differentiate approved public facts from internal-only or draft sources. If the account claims live features, dates, partners, numbers or token mechanics, verify at the registered source plus date. Missing approval -> placeholder or ask to verify, not confident promotion. User-provided positioning does NOT approve every product fact.
6. Ownership/routing: paired personal and official accounts (only if owner supplied), original-content categories and cross-account use permissions, credit/link policy and who reviews official claims.

Do not store real/private account profiles, performance exports or approved documents in the open-source Skill repository. Suggest `~/.claude/account-profiles/<account-id>.md` and `~/.claude/approved-sources/<account-id>.md` as *possible* private locations, but write only after explicit permission. Use `assets/approved-source-register-template.md` as a blank form.

## Applying the profile

- Read the selected Account Profile **before** recommending angles or formats. A trending topic does not trump the editorial positioning; if it is not a fit, say so.
- Verify vocabulary, claimed expertise, stance boundaries, approval/attribution sources, forbidden claims and allowed calls to action.
- For official accounts, use source citations/approval for organizational claims. For a personal account, its author's views remain sovereign, but never turn unverified employer product details into hard facts.
- If the account profile is absent, **draft provisionally** and avoid company-specific factual assertions. Ask at most one blocking question if account ownership, approval or distribution channel materially affects correctness. Offer optional positioning onboarding after the useful draft; do not pretend a profile exists.
- User's latest explicit approved change outranks old positioning. Never silently persist an inferred update.

## Reference: perspective before topic

A creator can write across multiple subjects if the account's underlying lens stays coherent. For inspiration on the value of a point of view over a rigid niche, read Dan Koe's published essay `https://thedankoe.com/letters/you-dont-need-a-niche-you-need-a-point-of-view/`. This is high-level strategy only, not a source for the specific X post shared by a user or permission to copy a contemporary creator's distinct language.
