# Writing Mode — how this piece speaks

Account positioning and the author's approved Voice Profile outrank temporary style presets. The mode controls *presentation*, not worldview, invented credentials or factual standards. Never rebrand the author as someone they aren't.

| Mode | What it means | Typical fit | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PROFESSIONAL** / 专业分析 | precise terminology, measured claims, clear causal chain, tight editing | institutional account, product/sector analysis | sterile PR voice, empty jargon |
| **CONVERSATIONAL** / 轻松交流 | natural cadence, direct observation, plain words, flexible paragraph shapes | personal observations and discussion | fake banter, forced slang, impersonated first-person story |
| **RESEARCH_DRIVEN** / 研究驱动 | clear method, provenance, uncertainty, competing explanations; readable narrative | evidence-rich Article or market deep dive | citations as decoration, overstated certainty |
| **OPINIONATED** / 鲜明观点 | explicit thesis, sharp distinctions, fair counterpoint, distinctive consequence | authored CT takes and QRTs | manufactured controversy, straw men, reckless certainty |
| **BUILDERS_NOTES** / 实践笔记 | concrete constraints, trade-offs, what changed in a workflow, lessons | build-in-public, operational retrospective | inventing implementation details, first-person achievements or outcomes |

**Independent axes:** Writing Mode ≠ Research Level ≠ Output Format. A CONVERSATIONAL Article can use R2 depth; a RESEARCH_DRIVEN thread may use R1. Research mode changes rhetoric and evidence presentation, not a guarantee that source-gathering tools exist. The user can combine any modes with ARTICLE, LONG_POST, SHORT_POST, THREAD or REWRITE.

**Selection logic:** Honor explicit mode. If user asks “what style?”, recommend one based on account and purpose and give 2–3 meaningful alternatives; do not present an obligatory onboarding form. If user is vague, infer a temporary mode from Account Profile and examples; do not save it without approval. On a simple quick draft avoid asking style questions. A request for “human” is a reason to inspect the voice/logic and sentence rhythms, *not* automatically switch to CONVERSATIONAL.

**Personal variation:** Use author-specific self-authored samples. A writer may be colloquial while evidence-driven or formal but funny. Preserve individual rhythm, idiosyncrasy, dialect, vocabulary and authentic uncertainty; do not apply identical “authentic” cadence to everyone.
