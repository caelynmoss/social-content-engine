# Voice distillation — evidence, thought patterns and editorial taste

The author's own final writing and corrections are stronger signals than popularity metrics or reference authors. **Do not assume personality from writing style.** Treat opinions as time-specific and revisable.

## Onboard from authentic samples

- 8–20 posts actually written by the author across topics; 3–5 can support a provisional style, not a claim of fidelity.
- An optional 2–4 peers' posts, **clearly tagged PEER**, usable only for broad craft techniques.
- Optional user-final versus AI-first draft pairs; these are valuable positive and negative examples.
- If only a handle is supplied, check public accessibility and ask for authorship confirmation. Never claim ownership from an X handle alone.
- Don't block a one-off writing request while collecting samples: draft now, invite calibration after.

## Profile should capture 4 distinct layers

1. **Expression mechanics:** average length *qualitatively*, line breaks, fragments, grammar, punctuation, language switching, opening, closing, hooks, imagery, humor, jargon, formatting, avoid list.
2. **Reasoning signature:** first-principles vs personal observation vs data-first, use of contrasts, qualifiers, examples, counterarguments, ability to say "I don't know", strength of conclusions.
3. **Editorial taste:** what the author finds worth a post, novelty threshold, preferred level of technical depth, situations where they avoid a hot take, topics they choose not to write about.
4. **Contextual lens:** recurring interests, audience, domain expertise and communication goals. Do **not** freeze any stance into permanent worldview; explicitly flag what is observed vs self-reported.

Every important trait requires an OWN sample index or an explicit author correction, not invented biography. An author's references and interests may change over time.

## Confidence labels

- **PROVISIONAL:** 0–4 valid OWN samples; write but do not assert stylistic match.
- **SUPPORTED:** 5–11 diverse OWN samples, recurring observable patterns; expose uncertainty.
- **CALIBRATED:** at least 12 OWN posts across formats **and** explicit user feedback or a holdout check. Still not guaranteed perfect.

More tweets are not automatically better. If examples contradict or cover only one genre, explain confidence gap.

## Multi-author privacy

Use separate author IDs and local private files where the author authorizes storage. Do not mix employees' prose or transpose a personal author's style onto official account communications automatically. Do not commit real samples into shared repo or the skill ZIP. Read `references/feedback-loop.md` for corrections and optional persistence.
