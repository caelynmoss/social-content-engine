# Thinking partner — deepen the idea before writing

Use when the user asks to talk through a thesis, sends rambling notes, or says an output is generic. Keep conversation dynamic rather than administering an interview.

**For short input use `references/idea-expansion.md`. A seed deserves actual mechanisms, stakes, and a fair counterexample, not longer prose repeating a slogan.** Honor a requested finished draft in the same turn; optional two-or-three-angle menu only when it helps the author choose.

## Infer the stage

- **Observation stage:** They noticed something, but have not decided what it implies. Surface 2–3 possible interpretations, not a fake conviction.
- **Position stage:** They have a thesis. Pressure-test one central assumption, then improve it without reversing the view.
- **Draft stage:** They know what they want to say. Prefer execution and concise follow-up, not another brainstorming loop.

## Questions that actually change the writing

Good: "Is your objection to *who can launch*, or to *who controls liquidity after launch*? Those lead to different posts."

Bad: "Who is your target audience? What tone? Which platform? What word count? What hashtags?" in one volley, when the user simply asked for a tweet.

Ask at most one question when the missing answer changes the thesis, factual accuracy, audience stakes, or moral force. Otherwise generate a provisional post, then ask whether the strongest point is right.

## Three genuinely different angles

Angle diversity is conceptual, not lexical. For a user's remark "AI agents are becoming economic actors":
- **Authority:** Agency means control over decisions, not merely a wallet or an identifier.
- **Accountability:** Autonomy without an observable decision trail gives holders a new black box.
- **Incentives:** Operational autonomy changes who captures value and who bears failure.

Choose only an angle supported by what the author actually meant. Label a speculative extension as your suggestion. Present trade-offs when the user requested brainstorming; do not automatically output all three on a normal posting request.

## Diagnosis when user says "not smart enough"

Classify the problem as (A) weak insight, (B) weak argument, (C) generic writing, (D) outdated/unverified facts, or (E) wrong author voice. Show a brief fix if asked. If no example is given, offer one practical illustration and ask for one bad output to calibrate; do not claim a generic rule has solved personal voice fidelity.
