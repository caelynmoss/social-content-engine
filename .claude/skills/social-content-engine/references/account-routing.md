# Content source and cross-account routing

Load paired account profiles only if the user has approved the relationship. An official account and a human creator have different editorial jobs. **Choose the origin and amplification path before drafting**, not after writing a generic tweet and reposting it everywhere.

## Configurable pattern for paired personal and official accounts

| Material | Default origin | Optional follow-on | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original judgment, founder perspective, firsthand story, hard-earned lesson, personal prediction | Personal account | Official account may **credit and expand** an idea into an institutional lens, with permission and a canonical source link | Do not present an individual's opinion as official policy; cite, don't copy |
| Product tutorial, verified data, release note, partnership, incident report, institutional announcement | Official account | Personal account only when it adds genuine *independent interpretation, experience or critique* | Avoid mechanical reposting, mirrored text or unsourced insider claims |
| Mixed analytical piece with new organizational evidence and personal interpretation | Choose using account objective, rights, approvals and existing distribution | Distinct follow-up angle if clearly useful | One primary source of truth, disclose personal opinion vs official fact |

The above is a **suggested pattern**, not a universal mandate for all users. An account owner may explicitly choose different origin/approval rules and record them in the account's private profile. If no paired official account exists, there is no cross-account workflow. The Skill itself never publishes, reposts, schedules, likes or creates coordinated engagement.

## Decision procedure

1. Identify content type: personal thought/story, data/tutorial/partner announcement, mixed, or user-specified.
2. Check each account's positioning/format evidence and intended audience; verify whether the content fits that account at all.
3. Check source and approval registry, particularly organizational product claims and private experiences.
4. Recommend **one originating account**, the most appropriate evidence-led format, and an optional **distinct** follow-on for another account. Don't generate duplicate posts for every account by default.
5. If follow-on is requested, attribute the origin, link it, *expand* substantively and respect the owner's permission. For official-to-personal, the personal author should contribute an independent take, not simply repost the announcement.
6. If a direct quote/repost is explicitly requested, draft copy only and explain what incremental value it brings. Do not treat distribution volume as an objective by itself.

## Measurement feedback

Keep original posts, quotes, reshares, official citations and article links separate in performance data; don't call repeated reposts new original work. Evaluate whether the content flow creates unique insights, qualified engagement and a clear source of truth. Account-level metrics from a user's private data must not be published in this open-source repository without permission.
