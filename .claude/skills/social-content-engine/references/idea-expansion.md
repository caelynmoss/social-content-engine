# Adaptive Idea Expansion — depth before prose

Run for a short, general, underspecified or contradictory idea. A vague input is an **invitation to think**, not permission to invent the author's stance. A one-line input should not automatically produce a one-line insight.

## Determine the real stage

- **Seed / observation:** the writer offers a subject, question or impression, but no defensible mechanism or position. Offer two or three *genuinely different* hypotheses, each with a potential mechanism, implication and one evidence gap. They are SUGGESTED directions, not claims the writer already endorses.
- **Thesis:** writer supplies a clear claim. Test its strongest assumption, plausible counterexample and practical relevance; preserve the original stance.
- **Draft:** writer already provided a coherent argument or explicitly asked for final copy. Avoid making them go through a workshop; deepen silently where possible, deliver the requested copy.

## Five probes: use the most relevant two, not a template

1. **Specific mechanism:** Who has what decision right, incentive, constraint, capital or information? What actually causes the outcome?
2. **Unexpected distinction:** Which two concepts are repeatedly treated as identical, but behave differently?
3. **Real-world stakes:** Who would act differently if the claim were true? What concrete decision changes?
4. **Counterexample:** When would the thesis not hold? What competing explanation deserves consideration?
5. **Missing observation:** What one datum, contract clause, firsthand detail, case or source would materially strengthen or falsify the angle?

## Default behavior

- For a generic seed such as “Agents aren't really autonomous”: offer up to **three distinct editorial paths** (e.g. decision authority, loss accountability, economic control), with a one-sentence mechanism and evidence needed for each. **Do not imply these are verified factual conclusions.** If user asked for a post, recommend a path and also supply one concrete provisional draft, rather than stopping at choices.
- If the user asks directly for an Article, enrich first, perform research to chosen depth, and write the Article in the same response when feasible; disclose any lack of evidence. Do not force a long question loop.
- If user's request is clearly “just draft it,” deepen silently and return the best draft first. Ask **at most one decisive question** only where author stance, account authority or fact accuracy otherwise cannot be respected.
- Each paragraph needs **new information or inferential movement**. Expanding word count without increasing insight is a failure; cutting repetition is preferred over artificial length.
- Do not invent user experience, recent X conversations, partner names, contract behavior, study findings, statistics or quotations to fill gaps.

## Optional selection moment

When the user explicitly asks to explore, or multiple plausible angles would produce materially different results, show a **small menu of 2–3 angles**, recommend one, and briefly offer writing mode + research level. This is not an obligatory form or persistent preference. Otherwise infer the best provisional choices from Account Profile and author request.
